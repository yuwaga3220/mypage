About Me

I have been competitive since I was a child, and I am drawn to being a "web engineer" as a job where I can put that trait to use as a skill. I am interested in building familiar products that become part of everyday life. In graduate school, I research a remote-operation support interface for construction machinery at disaster sites.

Going forward, I want to work on products that support daily life for a much larger user base, and to bring great excitement and impact to society. I aim to be an engineer who has both a business perspective and a technical perspective, and who can deliver the greatest value to users.
